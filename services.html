Personalized Training Program

No two dogs get the same plan. Neither will yours.

Based on your intake and our call, I build a structured, step-by-step program designed specifically for your dog's behavior patterns, learning style, and your daily life.

Your plan includes clear milestones, detailed exercises with written instructions, and the reasoning behind each step, so you understand what you're doing and why it works. That applies to everything from basic obedience and reactivity work to scent detection foundations. Because an owner who understands the approach gets results. An owner who's just following instructions guesses.

What's included:

  • Written training plan built for your specific dog
  • Milestone structure with clear progression markers
  • Explanation of the science and reasoning behind each step
  • Delivered digitally, reviewed together on a call

Format: Delivered digitally + review call · Price: 1 session $125, 2 sessions $240, 3 sessions $350. Use within 90 days.

Behavior Deep Dive

For when something specific is seriously wrong.

Dealing with reactivity, aggression, severe separation anxiety, or a fear response that's affecting daily life? This is a focused session built to go deep on one problem.

I'll walk through the full behavioral picture: triggers, history, context, escalation patterns. I'll identify the root causes and give you a targeted action plan with clear, specific steps to address it.

Best for: Owners dealing with a specific, acute behavioral issue that needs more than a general training plan.

What's included:

  • Follow-up Q&A access

Working Dog Training

Some dogs need a job. I can help you give them one.

Not every dog needs behavioral correction. Some dogs need purpose. Working dog training grows directly out of the same positive reinforcement and incremental shaping I use with every client. Same method. Different goal.

If your dog has drive, focus, and energy that regular walks and training don't fully channel, this is where we take that and build something with it.

Best for: High-drive dogs who need a job and owners exploring specialized training goals.

Training areas include:

  • Scent work and nose work foundations
  • Obedience and good manners in public
  • Drive building and handler focus

What's included:

  • Assessment of your dog's drive, aptitude, and temperament
  • Structured training plan built for your specific goal
  • Home visits available for hands-on work around Portland, OR
  • Ongoing support as behaviors are proofed and refined

Format: Home visits around Portland, OR + written plan + check-ins · Ask about pricing

Remote Consult

Live help over video when I can't get to your door.

Some dogs are too far for a home visit. Some owners just need another set of eyes between visits. We get on video, you show me what's going on, and I coach you through it as it happens.

Home visits around Portland are still how most of my training happens. This is for when that's not an option. It works well for pulling, jumping, barking at the door, a new puppy routine, or one problem you want a straight answer on.

Remote Consult isn't the right fit for aggression or bite-risk cases. Those need a home visit, and the free call is where we'll talk it through.

What's included:

  • 60-minute live video session, camera on so I can see your dog
