Services

in-home across the portland metro. on your screen from anywhere else.

Free consult

A free 30-minute consult, on video. We talk about your dog, what's working, what isn't. If we're a fit, we book a session. If not, I'll send you toward someone who is. No charge, no pitch, no contract.

graduation gift, self-awarded

Standard session · $125

75 minutes in your home. We pick one behavior, learn the technique together, and you leave with a plan you can run tomorrow. Best for: pulling, basic manners, jumping, mild reactivity.

multi-dog household, mid-negotiation

Premium session · $175

90 minutes in your home. For multi-dog households, separation anxiety groundwork, fear cases, dogs who need real decompression breaks mid-session, or houses where three humans need to walk out with the same playbook. Includes a written follow-up plan and one free check-in within the next two weeks.

attending class from the couch

Remote coaching · same prices

Not near Portland? Same sessions, over video. You bring the dog and a phone with a camera. I bring the plan. It works better than people expect: most of training is coaching the human anyway, and the human fits on a screen just fine. Standard and Premium, $125 and $175, same as in-home.
