Services

in-home sessions, in your neighborhood, with your actual dog.

Free consult

20-minute video call. We talk about your dog, what's working, what isn't. If we're a fit, we book a session. If not, I'll send you toward someone who is. No charge, no pitch, no contract.

Standard session · $125

75 minutes in your home. We pick one behavior, learn the technique together, and you leave with a plan you can run tomorrow. Best for: pulling, basic manners, jumping, mild reactivity.

Premium session · $175

90 minutes in your home. For multi-dog households, serious reactivity, fear cases, or anything that needs a longer working window. Includes a written follow-up plan and one free check-in within the next two weeks.
