# book.html — Page Overrides

Overrides MASTER for the booking page only.

- **Form is the focal point.** Page hero is short (one Instrument Serif line + one sentence). Push the Tally iframe above the fold on desktop; on mobile, form-first — minimize hero copy so the form is visible without scrolling past more than the header.
- **Snap-scroll:** REDUCE. Book page should scroll naturally — no `.snap-section` on form section or trust signals. Snap interrupts form completion.
- **Primary CTA:** the Tally submit button is the page's gold action. No competing gold buttons on the page. Header "Book" nav button stays gold (consistent global nav).
- **Trust signals:** "24hr reply / Kevin reads every submission / Free / Force-free" line stays directly under the form, not buried below FAQ.
- **"What Happens After You Submit" 4-step block:** keep but compress vertical padding — it's reassurance, not the hero.
- **Mobile:** form-first, FAQ collapsed by default, no decorative scroll-hint arrow below 768px.
