Training that treats your dog like an individual.

Most training fails because it treats every dog the same. Your dog isn't every dog.

The Dog Vibes is built on one idea: lasting change comes from understanding your specific dog — their history, their triggers, their learning style. And working with all of it. Not a program. Not a checklist. A real plan for your real dog.

I use classical conditioning and positive reinforcement. Force-free, science-backed, proven. No corrections, no intimidation. Just clear communication and a foundation built on trust.

From overwhelmed to confident. Here's the path.

  1. Step 1

    Tell me about your dog

    Fill out a short intake form. I want to know your dog's background, what you're dealing with, and what success looks like for you. Before we ever meet.

Why owners choose The Dog Vibes.

I spent a decade walking dogs across New York City. Every breed, every temperament, every behavioral challenge imaginable. That hands-on experience shapes everything I do.
