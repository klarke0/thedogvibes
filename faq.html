FAQ

everything you need to know about working with The Dog Vibes.

Do you work with puppies?

Yes. Starting early is one of the best investments you can make. Early training builds the foundation for everything that comes later. The earlier we start, the easier everything is.

My dog has serious aggression issues. Can you help?

Aggression is something I take seriously and approach carefully. In many cases it's workable with the right approach and realistic expectations. Book a Behavior Assessment first. We'll talk through the full picture before making any commitments.

Do you offer in-person sessions?

The Dog Vibes is primarily remote. If you're in the Portland, OR area, reach out. Limited in-person sessions may be available depending on the situation.

How long does training take?

It depends on what you're working on and how consistently you practice. Some goals are achievable in a few weeks. Others are longer arcs. I'll give you an honest timeline during our Discovery Consultation. I won't oversell a quick fix if it isn't realistic.

What methods do you use?

Force-free, always. Classical conditioning and positive reinforcement only. No punishment, no aversives, no dominance-based methods. Not because it's trendy. The research is clear and the results are better.

I've already tried another trainer. Is it too late?

Not at all. Many of my clients have tried one or two other trainers before The Dog Vibes. A fresh approach, one built around your specific dog, can make a big difference.

Do you train service or assistance dogs?

I work with owners on scent work, alert dog training, and assistance dog task training. Same positive reinforcement methodology, just applied to working dog goals. If you're exploring this for your dog, the Discovery Consultation is the right place to start.

How much does it cost?

The Discovery Consultation is free: a full 30-minute call with no obligation. Ongoing sessions run $125 for a Standard session or $175 for a Premium session, depending on what your dog needs. We'll go over pricing during your consultation so there are no surprises.

What if The Dog Vibes isn't the right fit?

That's exactly what the Discovery Consultation is for. We figure it out together. No pressure, no commitment. If I'm not the right match, I'll tell you honestly and point you in the right direction.
